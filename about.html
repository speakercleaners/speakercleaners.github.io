About Us

SpeakerCleaners is a free web tool built for one simple job: getting water and dust out of phone speakers without an app, a repair shop, or a bill.

The idea came from a problem almost every phone owner knows. A phone slips into a pool, gets caught in the rain, or spends months collecting pocket lint — and suddenly calls sound distant and music sounds dull. The usual fixes are slow (a bag of rice for a day), risky (blowing compressed air into a tiny grille) or expensive (a repair shop visit).

There is a better way, and it has been hiding in plain sight: sound itself. A speaker is built to move. When it plays a low, steady tone, its membrane shakes with enough force to throw off water droplets and dust sitting on it. Smartwatches have used this trick for years. We brought the same idea to the web, so any phone with a browser can do it.

What the tool does

  • Water Eject — a steady 165 Hz tone that shakes water out of the speaker grille.
  • Dust Clean — a sweep from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz that loosens dust, lint and sand, one frequency step at a time.
  • Deep Clean — a full cycle that combines the water tone, the dust sweep and vibration for stubborn cases.
  • Test — short test tones so you can hear whether your speaker sounds clear again.

You can also choose which speaker to clean — left, right, or both — because most phones have stereo speakers and usually only one side has the problem.

Who we are

We run the Phone Speaker Cleaner YouTube channel, where we publish speaker cleaner sounds at different frequencies. This website is the same idea in tool form: open the page, press start, and the sound does the work.

Our promise

The tool is free and will stay free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no locked features and no downloads. We do not collect your data, and we do not need it — the sound is generated on your own device, in your browser.

If the tool helped you, the best thanks is to share it with someone whose phone sounds muffled.
