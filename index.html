How the cleaning sound works

A speaker is a tiny drum. When it plays a low, steady tone, the membrane shakes hard — hard enough to throw off whatever is sitting on it. That is the whole idea behind this tool.

💧 Water ejection

A steady 165 Hz tone makes the speaker membrane move with strong, even strokes. The shaking pushes water droplets out through the grille, the same way smartwatches eject water.

🌀 Dust removal

Dust and lint sit in the tiny holes of the grille. A sweeping tone that moves from 200 Hz to 1500 Hz shakes each size of particle loose, one frequency step at a time.

📳 Vibration boost

On phones that support it, the deep-clean cycle adds vibration alongside the sound. The extra mechanical shaking helps with water or dust that sound alone could not move.

Clean your speaker in 5 steps

  1. Wipe the outside dry

    Use a soft, dry cloth to remove any water you can see on the phone.

  2. Turn the volume all the way up

    Stronger sound means stronger shaking. Set the volume to maximum before you start.

  3. Speaker facing down

    Put the phone on a flat surface with the speaker pointing down, so gravity helps the water fall out.

  4. Pick a mode and press start

    Choose Water Eject for moisture or Dust Clean for muffled, dusty sound. Pick left, right or both speakers.

  5. Test and repeat

    Play music or make a call. If the sound is still dull, run the cycle 2–3 times.

Frequently asked questions

Is the speaker cleaner sound safe for my phone?

Yes. The tool only plays tones in the normal audio range, the same kind of frequencies your speaker already plays when you listen to music. It does not open the phone or touch any part, so there is nothing to damage.

Which devices does it work on?

Any device with a speaker and a web browser: iPhone, Samsung and other Android phones, iPads, tablets, laptops and desktop computers. No app or installation is needed.

How long does one cleaning cycle take?

Water Eject runs for about 60 seconds, Dust Clean for about 45 seconds, and the full Deep Clean for about 90 seconds. For stubborn water or dust, run the cycle 2–3 times.

Do I need to install an app?

No. This is a web tool — open the page, press start, and the sound plays directly from your browser.

My speaker is still muffled after cleaning. What now?

Repeat the cycle a couple of times with the volume at maximum, and try Deep Clean. If it is still muffled, the speaker may have physical damage or dried residue inside, and a repair shop should look at it.

Can it remove salt water?

It can eject salt water, but salt water needs fast action: use the tool immediately, then wipe the outside with a damp cloth of fresh water (only if your phone is water-resistant) and repeat. Salt left inside can corrode parts over time.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. There are no subscriptions, no premium modes and no payments anywhere on this site.

How often should I clean my speaker?

Whenever the sound gets dull or after any water exposure. As routine care, a quick clean once a week keeps the speaker in good shape.

Why can I choose left, right or both speakers?

Most phones have stereo speakers. If only one side got wet or dusty, cleaning just that side saves time. Both is the best choice for a general clean.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, the cleaning tones are generated on your device, so the tool keeps working even if your connection drops.

Tips for the best clean

🔊 Volume matters

Always clean at maximum volume. Louder sound shakes the speaker harder and moves more water and dust.

⬇️ Let gravity help

Speaker facing down on a flat surface works far better than holding the phone in your hand.

🧻 Dry first

Wipe visible water off before you start, so the sound can work on the moisture trapped inside.

⏱️ Act fast

Use the tool as soon as the phone gets wet. The longer water sits inside, the deeper it goes.

🔁 Repeat the cycle

One pass is often enough, but a second or third pass with Deep Clean removes what the first one missed.

🎧 Test between cycles

Play a song or make a call after each cycle to hear the improvement and decide if another round is needed.
